Open source World Model engine

Create infinite experiences, in minutes.

Your World Model needs a dungeon master.

Like a tabletop dungeon master, GENESIS remembers your inventory, characters and locations, applies the rules and connects player actions to generated scenes. It gives your World Model the memory, shared state and playable interface an infinite experience needs.

What GENESIS handles

Your controller owns the world's facts. Your LLM and World Model receive the context for what happens next, and every player's interface reflects the accepted result.

World memory

Persist inventory, health, quest flags, locations and character relationships in structured state.

Playable worlds

Build custom interfaces, authoritative turns, player seats and crowd decisions around generated scenes.

Connected generation

Validate LLM replies, track asynchronous jobs and carry real scene checkpoints into the next moment.

Describe any world. Start with one prompt.

The included create-world-stream skill helps a coding agent turn your idea into a working prototype: world settings, a custom interface, controller rules, assets and an exportable bundle.

Use the create-world-stream skill to build Mosslight, a forest
battle worldstream with a cream pixel interface. Let two players
take turns, ask the backend LLM for four moves per encounter,
and remember their inventory, health and progress. Give me a
working local preview and an exportable world.

Start with labeled fixtures for a local prototype. Connect your chosen models and a generation budget for live media. Learn the agent workflow.

Three files. Your whole world.

my-world/
├── genesis.json      World settings, actions, tools and assets
├── interface.html    Your interface and player controls
├── controller.js     World memory, rules and backend requests
└── assets/           Images, audio and video that travel with you

GENESIS coordinates the world; connected providers generate media. Your host supplies identity, storage, credentials and spending policy. Start building.

Choose your connection

Create and publish with Sequencer

Open My Worlds, sign in with your Sequencer account and create a world. Describe your idea to Spark, or import a GENESIS project in the world editor. Preview it, save it and publish it to Worldstreams.

Your existing account, workspace, assets and gems carry through creation and publishing. Exploring these docs and watching the recorded demos is free.
